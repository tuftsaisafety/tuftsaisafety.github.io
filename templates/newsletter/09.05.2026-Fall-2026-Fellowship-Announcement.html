Fall Fellowship Applications Now Open

To the TASSA community,

Welcome back! I hope everybody had a rewarding summer. We are very happy to share that:

Our fall fellowship applications are now open! Apply here by September 15th, 2026 (end of day, midnight EDT).

Our fellowships are 8-week, in-person reading groups that meet for 1.5hrs/wk. Fellows can participate in one or both of the following:

  • Technical Fellowship: Discussion surrounding the foundations of advanced AI systems and the latest AI Safety research, including model interpretability, goal misgeneralization, reinforcement learning, scalable oversight, red-teaming, and more.
  • Policy Fellowship: Discussion of major decisions and developments in AI governance, responsible scaling, compute governance, deepfakes & misinformation, international frameworks, and more.

Open to undergraduate and graduate students. Catered lunch/dinner from local restaurants is provided.

No prior experience required — we encourage anyone to apply! Learn more on our website.

Questions? Email contact@tassa.dev.

The general interest meeting is on Wednesday, September 9th at 7:00pm in 302 Cabot Center. Come and let the organizers get to know you! So much has happened in AI safety over these past four months, and we’re itching to talk to you all about it.
