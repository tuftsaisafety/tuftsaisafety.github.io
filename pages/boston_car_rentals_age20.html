Boston Car Rental Policies (Age 20)

Summary of primary rental company age limits and restrictions for drivers aged 20.

Status Company Policy & Specifics
Allows (Age 20) Hertz Minimum age 20 nationwide (economy through full-size). Young driver fee waived or discounted for AAA members.
Allows (Age 20) Enterprise Minimum age 20 across Massachusetts, including Boston Logan (BOS) and neighborhood locations. Daily surcharge applies.
Allows (Age 20) National Minimum age 20 (shares fleet with Enterprise). Specialty and luxury tiers restricted.
Allows (Age 20) Alamo Minimum age 20 (Enterprise Holdings network). Standard young driver surcharge.
Allows (Age 20) Dollar Minimum age 20 nationwide (Hertz subsidiary). Daily young driver fee applies.
Allows (Age 20) Thrifty Minimum age 20 nationwide (Hertz subsidiary). Standard young driver fee.
Allows (Age 20) Sixt Minimum age 20 for select standard categories (compact, sedan). High-end/luxury tiers require age 21 or 25+.
Does Not Allow Avis Minimum age is 21 nationwide (exceptions only in NY and MI mandated by state law).
Does Not Allow Budget Minimum age is 21 nationwide (same policy and exceptions as Avis).

Note: Most companies charge an underage surcharge ($20–$35/day) for drivers under 25 and require a major credit card at pickup.
